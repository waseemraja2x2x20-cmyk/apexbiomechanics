    How the angle and grades work

    The cervical angle here is the 2D ear-to-shoulder line against upward vertical: 0° is directly above the shoulder. Grade 1: below 15°; grade 2: 15° to below 30°; grade 3: 30° to below 45°; grade 4: 45° or more. These are app-defined feedback bands, not validated medical thresholds.

    Average is weighted by visible tracking time; worst is the largest valid angle. We use the more visible side, reject low-confidence landmarks and ask you to turn sideways. Camera perspective, clothing and lighting affect the estimate. At least 5 seconds of usable tracking is required. No force, pressure, muscle activation or diagnosis is measured.

    Rep detection thresholds

    Squat: knee angle ≤100° down, ≥160° up. Push-up: elbow angle ≤90° down, ≥155° up. Each position must hold for 200ms. A rep needs an extended start, a down position, and a return up. Tracking loss resets the current partial rep. Depth is a visual flexion estimate, not a measure of safe range.
